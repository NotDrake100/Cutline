A heartbreaking incident unfolded on BT Kawade Road, Pune, on September 25 around 4 PM. A 20-year-old youth, Vignesh Santosh Pawar from Ramtekdi, lost his life after losing balance while attempting to board a moving tractor-trolley returning from Ganesh Visarjan near Kalubai Chowk. He fell and was fatally injured when the trolley's rear wheel ran over him. Doctors at Sassoon Hospital declared him dead on arrival, and the body was subsequently sent to Command Hospital for autopsy.
